Job Safety Tips

Protect Yourself from Fake Jobs

1. Never Pay Registration Fees

Genuine employers generally do not ask applicants to pay money to get a job.

2. Verify the Company

Check the company's official website and contact details.

3. Be Careful with Advance Payments

Avoid sending money for security deposits or job confirmation.

4. Check Email Addresses

Compare the sender's email address with the company's official domain.

5. Check Job Advertisements

Be careful with unrealistic salaries and guaranteed-job promises.

6. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not open unknown links or share personal information on unverified websites.
