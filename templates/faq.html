Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Fake Job Advertisement Detector?

It is a cybersecurity application that checks job advertisements for suspicious indicators.

2. How does this application work?

It analyzes advertisement text, detects selected warning signs and calculates a risk score.

3. What is a Risk Score?

A score from 0 to 100 based on the warning indicators detected by the application.

4. What does High Risk mean?

It means multiple or high-scoring warning indicators were detected. Further verification is recommended.

5. Does Low Risk mean the job is genuine?

No. A low score does not guarantee that a job is genuine.

6. Does the application save analysis history?

Yes. Advertisement analysis results are stored in an SQLite database.

7. Is this application free?

Yes. This educational project is free to use locally.
