About Our Project

Fake Job Advertisement Detector

Fake Job Advertisement Detector is a cybersecurity project designed to identify suspicious job advertisements.

Project Purpose

Our purpose is to help job seekers recognize warning signs in online job advertisements and avoid potential job scams.

Project Objectives

  • Detect suspicious job advertisement keywords.
  • Identify suspicious shortened URLs.
  • Detect suspicious contact information.
  • Calculate advertisement risk scores.
  • Store analysis results in a database.

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Flask
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • SQLite Database

Project Goal

To promote safer online job searching through basic cybersecurity awareness and automated analysis.
